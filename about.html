About Dart

Built for the streets.
Fair pay for riders, real certainty for senders.

Dart was built to solve everyday logistics headaches: endless calls, unpredictable pricing, and unaccountable deliveries. We connect senders directly with trusted independent riders with full transparency.

🎯

Our Mission

To simplify urban logistics by replacing chaos with clarity. We provide individuals and local merchants with instant access to verified riders while ensuring riders earn honest, reliable income with total dignity and transparent pricing.

Our Vision

To become the heartbeat of last-mile delivery across African cities. A dependable platform where every package is accounted for, riders build thriving independent careers, and local businesses grow with complete confidence.

Stress, guesswork, and endless phone calls.

  • ✕ Calling multiple dispatchers to negotiate random rates.
  • ✕ No live map or tracking — wondering where your package is.
